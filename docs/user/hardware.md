# Hardware

Eligibility is capability-based. A receiver child gains a writable route only
when its exact source-validated identity, report family, active generation, and
transport authority agree.

## Supported Boundary

The generated [supported-hardware reference](../generated/supported-hardware.md)
separates operational routes from research-only candidates. The two physically
assured devices are fixtures for end-to-end evidence, not a runtime allowlist.

## Direct Devices

An exact matching direct USB device takes precedence over its receiver-backed
route. Unrelated devices and sibling routes remain independent.

## Unknown Devices

Pairing inventory alone is not presence or write authority. Unknown,
unqualified, stale, or unavailable children fail closed and remain visible in
diagnostics without being impersonated as supported controllers.
