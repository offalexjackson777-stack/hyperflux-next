# HyperFlux V2 Physical Fixture Acceptance

HyperFlux Next 2.0 was exercised with a Basilisk V3 Pro 35K mouse and a
DeathStalker V2 Pro TKL keyboard paired through a HyperFlux V2 receiver. These
two devices are physical assurance fixtures, not a runtime allowlist.

## Verified Boundary

- Both children were discovered through capability-based receiver profiles and
  appeared once in stock OpenRazer, Polychromatic, and OpenRGB.
- Ordinary input and the tested DPI, scroll, media, lighting, battery, charging,
  route-return, wired-duplicate, receiver-return, and sleep/wake paths remained
  usable through the qualified application boundary.
- Device off/on cycles withdrew and republished only the affected child while
  preserving its sibling.
- Stock applications retained their own device semantics and presentation;
  HyperFlux transported their bounded feature reports.
- Restoration remained disabled.

## Limits

- This evidence does not physically qualify another device or hardware variant.
- Source-qualified devices without equivalent physical testing remain in their
  separate assurance tier.
- Polychromatic Ripple has no route-associated key event source through the
  feature-only endpoint and remains unsupported.
- OpenRGB's colour-variant label can be inaccurate for this keyboard over both
  direct USB and HyperFlux; it is a cosmetic upstream limitation.

Detailed records containing local machine or session identifiers are not part of
the public repository. Public code, source-bound profiles, deterministic tests,
and this bounded result summary support the claims made in user documentation.
